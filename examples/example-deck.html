Chapter 2 · Lessons

What we would keep

The big one

Ship something real every single week.

Nothing aligned the team like a working artifact you could click through on a Friday.

One page of scope

If it did not fit on a page, we did not understand it yet.

Mornings for the hard part

A third of the work happened before 10 a.m. We protected that.

Cut, don't defer

"Later" is a graveyard. We removed features outright.

Chapter 2 · Principles

Two lists we kept on the wall

Do
  • Decide in the room. Async is for reporting, not deciding.
  • Prefer the boring tool. One HTML file beats a toolchain.
  • Show, don't tell. A demo settles most arguments.
Avoid
  • Silent scope creep. New work needs old work removed.
  • Status theatre. If it isn't running, it isn't done.
  • Premature abstraction. Wait for the third copy.
